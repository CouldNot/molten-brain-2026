01 · CHECK-IN

One question a day.

“Migraine today?” Yes logs pain, location, aura, nausea, light and sound sensitivity, and medication. No logs sleep, stress, and caffeine.

02 · TRACKING TASK

Neon watches your eyes.

During a 3–5 minute task, Pupil Labs Neon glasses track gaze, pupil size, blink rate, and squinting. Their motion sensor tracks head movement.

03 · VOICE NOTE

Say how you feel.

30–60 seconds, once a day. Scored on-device.

04 · WEARABLE

Settings made for you.

A neck-worn device built on DataFeel haptic disks uses vibration aimed at easing migraine pain. Frequency, intensity, and session length are tuned to you, inside hard safety limits.

05 · BRAIN VIEW

See the pathway.

A 3D brain in Vision Pro shows the targeted nerve pathway and your progress over time. It’s an illustration, not a brain scan.
