# 3.99.1 (2026-10-11)

## Data
- Refreshed the PDSP binding-affinity database to its October 2026 export: 31 drugs see updated Ki values (mostly antipsychotics such as brexpiprazole, cariprazine, iloperidone and quetiapine, from newly published PDSP-certified human assays), 30 new measured binding sites appear and 4 weak ones drop out. Paliperidone keeps all its assays measured under its other name, 9-hydroxyrisperidone. (7e7e847, 68038fd)
