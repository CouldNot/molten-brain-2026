# 3.115.1 (2026-10-13)

## Data

- Olanzapine + Samidorphan's two active metabolites now carry an orange "contradicted" pill: Stahl lists N-dealkylated samidorphan and samidorphan N-oxide as acting on the mu opioid receptor, while the US Lybalvi label states "Neither metabolite contributes to the pharmacological effects of samidorphan". Both are kept and the label sentence is quoted in the pill's tooltip (4e2d8a5, a477269, f22179a)

Les deux sont conservés et la phrase de la notice est citée dans l'infobulle de la pastille (4e2d8a5, a477269, f22179a)
