# 3.94.0 (2026-10-10)

## Data
- 15 new pathways now show where the brain's chemical messenger systems actually reach. Serotonin from the raphe now reaches the cerebellum, medulla, septum and olfactory bulb. Noradrenaline from the locus coeruleus now reaches the pons, midbrain and medulla. Dopamine from the ventral tegmental area now reaches the temporal and cingulate cortex, the thalamus, the hypothalamus, the raphe and the locus coeruleus. Acetylcholine from the pons now reaches the ventral tegmental area and the substantia nigra. Each arrow is backed by a sentence from Kandel or Nieuwenhuys that names both ends. Each quote was found verbatim on its page and confirmed by a separate model judge. Fewer regions now carry a transmitter's receptors with no pathway of that transmitter reaching them.
